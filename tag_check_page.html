Picture tag check

What this is. The computer looked at every picture and wrote down what it saw. A Short will pick its pictures from these words, so they need to match the picture.
  1. Look at a picture and read its tags. The first tag (outlined) is the main subject. The red dot is where the camera will aim. Printed on it says if the picture has big text, a logo or an age-rating card: such a picture is kept out of a Short unless a beat asks for it.
  2. If the tags or the dot are wrong, press Fix this, correct the words, and click on the picture to move the dot. Press Keep fix.
  3. When you are done, press Save my fixes at the top. A file named downloads.
  4. Move that file into (say yes if Windows asks to replace the old one).
Your fixes always win over the computer's tags. If everything looks right, there is nothing to save. You don't need to check all of them: a quick look at 20 or 30 is enough to see if the tags can be trusted.
