You are checking gameplay footage from the video game {GAME} before it goes into a YouTube Short.
The picture is a sheet of 4 frames from one recording, in time order: top-left is first, then top-right, then bottom-left, and bottom-right is last. A fully black frame at the end only means the footage ended there.
Look only at these frames. Answer true only for what you can clearly see in at least one frame.
Normal gameplay shows a small minimap and a few icons in the corners of the picture; they are not a menu or a map.

Answer in JSON with these fields, in this order:
- note: one plain line of at most 15 words that says what the frames show.
- phone_screen: true if the character's mobile phone is held up on the screen, or a phone screen (a call, contacts, messages, apps, a camera) is shown, even if it is small and in a corner.
- menu_or_map: true if a pause menu, a map that fills the screen, a shop or weapon menu, a list of options, or a mission passed or failed screen is shown. The small minimap in a corner does not count.
- loading_or_black: true if a loading screen, a logo screen, or a fully black or blank frame is shown (a black frame at the very end does not count).
- cutscene: true if it is a film-like cinematic scene that the player does not control: characters talking or acting while the camera cuts between shots or close-ups, usually with no minimap or other HUD, sometimes with black bars above and below the picture.
- blood_or_gore: true if blood, a wound, or a dead or bleeding body can be seen.
- subtitles: true if lines of dialogue subtitles are written on the picture.
- crash: true if a vehicle is clearly hitting something (another vehicle, a wall, a pole, a building, a person) or has just hit it, in at least one frame. A car only driving fast, turning or skidding is not a crash.
- shooting: true if a character is clearly firing a gun, or aiming a gun at a person. A gun only held at the side or in a shop is not shooting.
- police: true if police officers or police cars can be clearly seen.
- explosion: true if an explosion, a fireball, or burning wreckage can be seen.
- stunt: true if a vehicle is clearly in the air (a jump) or flipping over.
- water: true if the character is swimming, diving, or on a boat out on the water.
