You are logging gameplay from the video game {GAME}, so that a video editor can find footage later.
The picture is a sheet of 4 frames from one recording, in time order: top-left is first, then top-right, then bottom-left, and bottom-right is last. The frames are a second or two apart. A fully black frame means the recording had ended.
Look only at these frames. Do not guess what happened before or after them.

Answer in JSON with these fields, in this order:
- notable: one plain line of at most 15 words that says what happens in these frames. Name what an editor would search for when you can see it: a crash, an explosion, a gun drawn, a body on the ground, police, a stunt, a stolen car. Say only what you can see, and never list what is missing.
- activity: what the player is doing. Exactly one of:
  driving = the player is in or on a road vehicle (car, truck, van, motorbike, bicycle)
  chase = the player is in a vehicle and police or enemies are clearly chasing or shooting at it
  shootout = the player is on foot and is aiming or firing a gun, or is being shot at
  on_foot = the player is walking, running or standing, and is not aiming a gun
  flying = the player is in a plane or helicopter, or is parachuting
  boat = the player is in a boat or on a jet ski
  swimming = the player is in the water without a vehicle
  cutscene = a cinematic scene: close-ups of characters or a film-like camera, with no minimap and no other game HUD
  menu_map = a pause menu, a full-screen map, a shop menu, a mission passed or failed screen, or any other menu
  phone = the character's phone is held up large on the screen
  loading = a loading screen, or a black or almost empty screen
  other = none of the above
  If the frames differ, choose shootout or chase when at least one frame clearly shows it. Otherwise choose what most of the frames show.
- vehicle: the kind of vehicle the player is in or on: car, sports car, truck, van, motorbike, bicycle, boat, helicopter, plane. Use "none" if the player is not in a vehicle.
- police_visible: true only if police officers, police cars or police helicopters themselves can be seen in a frame. Wanted stars or flashing lights on the HUD alone do not count.
- confidence: how sure you are of the activity, from 0 to 1.
