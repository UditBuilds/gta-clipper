The image is a contact sheet of 8 frames taken at even steps across the clip, in time order: left to right along the top row, then left to right along the bottom row. The label above each frame is its number (1-8) and its time in the recording (minutes:seconds). The frame labelled PEAK is the loudest, busiest moment the clip picker found.

HUD hints: in normal gameplay the minimap is in the bottom-left corner. Wanted stars in the top-right corner mean the police are after the player.

First write "reason": one short sentence saying what happens across the frames.
Then "player_stalled": true if the player's car or character is stopped, stuck or barely moving in most of the frames; otherwise false.
Then "category", exactly one of:
- stunt_or_explosion: a jump, a flip, a vehicle in the air, an explosion or fire.
- chase_or_shootout: a police or gang chase, a getaway, a shootout or a fight. Fast driving with wanted stars counts as a chase.
- other_action: other exciting gameplay that fits neither of the above.
- clumsy_crash_or_stuck: an accidental, low-speed bump into traffic, a wall, a gate or a pole, getting stuck, or turning around awkwardly, with no chase or fight going on.
- boring_driving_or_walking: ordinary driving in traffic, walking or standing around, with nothing exciting happening.
- menu_or_cutscene: a pause menu, map, phone screen, shop menu, loading screen, or a cutscene (cinematic camera, no HUD).

If you are unsure between an action category and one of the last three, choose the action category: a good clip that is wrongly rejected costs more than a dull clip that is kept.
